# Black Poppy Magik & Apothecary

## Product philosophy
This is not generic merch. Products are useful, beautiful objects from the Black Poppy world. Preserve existing approved artwork and assign it deliberately product-by-product. Do not stamp the same logo across every object.

## Collections

### The Ritual Table
- Candles — signature collection
- Ritual cards
- Cutting/working board
- Apron
- Physical apothecary pieces

### The Working Grimoire
- Vegan leather journals
- Soft-cover journals
- Book of Shadows material
- Herb/reference books
- Sigil books
- Herb/sigil coloring books

### The Black Poppy Desk
- 16 × 32 XL desk mat — hero/signature product family
- Smaller desk mats where useful
- Cards
- Notebooks
- Objects designed to live beside the Flat/Grimoire

### Objects & Talismans
- Ornaments / seasonal balls and alternate ornament formats
- Phone cases / MagSafe tough cases
- Totes
- Carry pieces
- Smaller designed objects

### Black Poppy Editions
Limited, artwork-led releases. These may exist because the design deserves to exist; not everything must be evergreen.
